### 2. The Hartwell Building

**The central mystery.** A building downtown with a missing floor, temporal anomalies, and decades of cover-ups. X-Files meets local real estate drama.

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Built** | 1923 by Magnus Hartwell |
| **Magnus Disappeared** | 1931 (body never found) |
| **Current Owner** | Omnicorp Holdings (shell company) |
| **Official Floors** | 12 (but 1952 photos show 13) |
| **The Problematic Floor** | Floor 7 |

**Timeline of Incidents:**
- **1952**: Photographs show 13 floors, building now has 12
- **1967**: UFO sighting above building (later retracted)
- **1984**: Floor 7 "renovated" - basement under renovation ever since
- **1999**: "Temporal anomalies" reported in server room
- **2019**: Security footage shows figure that appears on no other cameras
- **2024**: Anonymous tip: "The mirrors are doors"
- **2025**: NestFinder listing for Floor 7 appeared/disappeared within 6 hours

**The Mirrors:** Floor 7 has mirrors that "shouldn't be looked at too long." Multiple witnesses report seeing things in them.

**Where It Appears:**
- WikiKnow: Article on the building's history
- ForChan: /x/ conspiracy threads
- DailyBuzz: Occasional news coverage
- NestFinder: Suspicious listings near the building
- OddsOracle: Betting on whether it will be developed
- HartwellFiles: Dedicated conspiracy archive site

**Cross-Reference Template:**
> "I moved out of my apartment near the Hartwell Building. The rent was great but I kept hearing this... static that breathes? And my mirrors were wrong."
